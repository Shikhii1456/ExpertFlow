The Industrial Problem: Why Handovers Fail

Current Industry Reality
"Unit Normal" Logbook Trap: Outgoing operators write 3 words, omitting that they trimmed cooling bypass by 5% or heard a slight gravel crackle.
Lost Reasoning ("What" without "Why"): Incoming shift sees a throttled valve or disabled alarm but has no idea *why* the previous shift took that action.
Uneven Shadowing & Shift Disparity: Shift A has a 30-year guru; Shift C consists of junior recruits. Plant trips occur disproportionately during Night Shift transitions.
